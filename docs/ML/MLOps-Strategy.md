<span># MLOps Strategy</span>
<span></span>
<span>## What is MLOps?</span>
<span></span>
<span>**MLOps (Machine Learning Operations)** is how we manage, deploy, and monitor models in production.</span>
<span></span>
<span>Think of it as DevOps for ML:</span>
<span>- Versioning = Track every model</span>
<span>- Monitoring = Watch for problems</span>
<span>- Retraining = Keep models fresh</span>
<span></span>
<span>&gt; **Why this matters:** Without MLOps, models degrade over time and you won't know until users complain.</span>
<span></span>
<span>---</span>
<span></span>
<span>## For Different Team Members</span>

<span>| Role | What to Look For |</span>
<span>|------|------------------|</span>
<span>| **ML Engineer** | Retraining pipeline, versioning |</span>
<span>| **DevOps** | Deployment, monitoring |</span>
<span>| **Project Manager** | Retraining schedule, SLAs |</span>
